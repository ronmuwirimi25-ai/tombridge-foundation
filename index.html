Our Mission

To serve and support cancer patients and their families with compassion, dignity and hope.

TomBridge Foundation is being developed around the belief that people facing life's most difficult moments deserve kindness, understanding and support. We aim to explore practical ways to make the cancer journey less isolating for patients and those who care for them.

Compassion

Recognising the emotional and personal challenges experienced by patients and their families.

Dignity

Keeping people at the heart of our vision, with respect for their individual circumstances and experiences.

Hope

Working towards a future where care, encouragement and human connection can make a difference.

The Heart Behind TomBridge

Some of life's deepest losses can inspire a lasting commitment to help others. TomBridge Foundation grew from the memories of Thomas and Bridget, whose lives and experiences with cancer left a profound mark.

Their memory is at the heart of this developing vision: to turn loss into impact and love into compassionate action for people facing difficult circumstances.

Forever remembered

In Loving Memory of Thomas and Bridget

Your lives remain part of our story. Your memory inspires the hope we seek to share.

Our Vision for the Future

We are at the beginning of this journey. As TomBridge Foundation develops, we hope to explore meaningful support for cancer patients and their families, build connections with others who share this vision, and identify opportunities to serve with care.

Our intended activities and partnerships will be developed responsibly as the organisation takes its next steps. We will share further information as plans become clearer.
