Our Mission

TomBridge Foundation is being developed with a commitment to serving and supporting people affected by cancer. We believe that nobody should have to face life's most difficult moments without compassion, understanding and hope.

Compassionate Support

Recognising the emotional challenges faced by patients and the people who care for them.

Dignity and Care

Promoting a caring approach that respects every person's circumstances, needs and dignity.

Hope Through Action

Working towards practical ways to make difficult experiences less isolating for patients and families.

Our Story

TomBridge Foundation was inspired by the lives and memories of Thomas and Bridget, whose experiences with cancer left a lasting impact.

Their memory inspires a vision of turning personal loss into meaningful support for others. TomBridge represents a commitment to carrying love forward through compassion, service and care for people facing difficult circumstances.

In Loving Memory

Thomas and Bridget

Your lives remain part of our story. Your memory inspires the hope we seek to share.
