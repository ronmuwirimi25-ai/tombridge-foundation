Discover Our Purpose

Welcome to TomBridge Foundation

Cancer affects more than a person's health. It can touch family life, emotional wellbeing, everyday responsibilities and a person's sense of security.

At TomBridge Foundation, we believe care should recognise the whole person. Behind every diagnosis is an individual with a life, a family, hopes and worries. Behind every person receiving treatment may be relatives and friends trying to remain strong while navigating uncertainty of their own.

Our vision is to grow into a compassionate source of practical help, understanding and connection for people facing these challenges. Support cannot remove every difficulty, but listening carefully, treating people with dignity and helping them find appropriate support can make a meaningful difference.

“A difficult chapter does not have to be the end of someone's story. Together, we can help make room for dignity, support and hope.”

Our Mission

To serve and support cancer patients and their families with compassion, dignity and hope.

TomBridge Foundation is being developed with the aim of relieving some of the practical and emotional pressures experienced by people affected by cancer. We hope to explore practical assistance, compassionate listening, helpful information and connections to appropriate health, welfare and community services.

Our intended work is grounded in a simple principle: people deserve to be treated as people first, not defined by a diagnosis or by the difficulties they face.

Our working charitable purpose
To promote health and relieve the needs of people affected by cancer, for the public benefit, through intended practical assistance, emotional support, information and signposting to appropriate services. This working description may be refined as the foundation develops its governance and registration plans.

How We Hope to Help

Support that sees the person, not just the diagnosis.

Every person's circumstances are different. Our future approach will need to listen to patients, families and trusted professionals to understand where support is most useful and how it can be delivered responsibly.

01 · Everyday needs

Practical Assistance

We hope to explore ways to ease everyday pressures that can arise during cancer treatment, such as difficulties with essential costs, travel to appointments or other practical needs. Any assistance would depend on future resources, criteria and safeguarding arrangements.

02 · Being heard

Emotional Support

A cancer diagnosis can bring fear, uncertainty and isolation. We want to foster a compassionate approach where people are listened to without judgement and, where appropriate, connected with qualified counselling, peer-support or specialist services.

03 · Finding a way forward

Information and Signposting

Finding reliable help can feel overwhelming. We aim to help people navigate information and connect with established health, welfare and community organisations, without replacing medical advice or specialist care.

04 · Care beyond the patient

Families and Carers

Family members and carers may carry practical and emotional responsibilities of their own. Our vision includes recognising their needs and helping them find appropriate support, information and moments of relief.

These are intended areas of work, not services currently available. Details will be published only when the foundation is ready and able to provide them.

The Story Behind TomBridge

A name shaped by love, remembrance and the desire to make a difference.

TomBridge Foundation is rooted in the memory of Thomas and Bridget, whose lives and loss through cancer are part of the reason this vision exists.

Grief changes the way we see the world. It can leave questions that have no easy answers and a deep awareness of how much people need care when life becomes difficult. In remembering loved ones, we may also discover a desire to turn what we have experienced into something that serves others.

The name TomBridge brings Thomas and Bridget together in one enduring name. It represents a bridge between remembrance and action, between people facing difficult moments and the support that can help them move forward, and between the pain of loss and the possibility of meaningful impact.

Our Approach

Compassion should be more than a promise. It should shape how people are treated.

  1. Listen first.
    Seek to understand each person's circumstances, priorities and wishes rather than assume everyone's needs are the same.
  2. Respect every person's dignity.
    Protect privacy, treat people fairly and communicate with kindness, care and respect.
  3. Connect people with appropriate help.
    Work towards clear signposting and responsible partnerships with relevant health, welfare and community organisations.
  4. Act responsibly with resources.
    Develop transparent processes, appropriate checks and clear criteria before any support or fundraising activity begins.
  5. Learn and improve.
    Listen to feedback and, when services are established, work towards understanding and reporting the difference they make.

Our Mission

To serve and support cancer patients and their families with compassion, dignity and hope.

TomBridge Foundation is being developed around the belief that people facing life's most difficult moments deserve kindness, understanding and support. We aim to explore practical ways to make the cancer journey less isolating for patients and those who care for them.

Our working mission is to help relieve some of the practical and emotional pressures that can affect people living with cancer and their families, while connecting them with appropriate specialist and community support.

How We Hope to Help

Support that sees the person, not just the diagnosis.

As the foundation develops, we hope to build a thoughtful and responsible approach around the needs of patients and families. The areas below describe our intended direction; they are not services currently available.

Practical Assistance

Explore ways to help with everyday pressures connected to a cancer journey, subject to available resources, fair eligibility criteria and appropriate safeguards.

Emotional Support

Help people feel less alone by identifying compassionate listening opportunities and signposting to established support organisations and specialist services.

Information and Signposting

Help patients and families find relevant NHS, cancer-support, welfare and community information from trusted organisations.

Families and Carers

Recognise the important role of family members and carers, whose own practical and emotional needs can also be significant.

TomBridge Foundation does not provide medical advice or treatment. Any future activities will be developed carefully and within the organisation's resources, responsibilities and legal status.
